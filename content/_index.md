### About

{{< figure src="/images/me.png" alt="Black and white stylized profile picture of Prodromos Kolyvakis" class="fig-center" >}}

I am the co-founder and CTO of [Aidvo](https://www.aidvo.co/), where we
are building a platform for booking elder care and nursing at home: vetted
caregivers, transparent pricing, and AI that lets families describe what they
need in their own words and find the right care in a few steps.

Before Aidvo I spent a decade turning machine learning research into shipped
products. My [Ph.D. from EPFL](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/record/277801)
focused on representation learning for *NLP* and knowledge graphs; since then
I have led applied AI work in [smart home tech](https://alexa.amazon.com),
[ad tech](https://www.taboola.com), [fintech](https://www.ai2c.ch),
[clinical research](https://www.chuv.ch/en/bdsc/research/our-groups/clinical-data-science),
and [medical virtual reality](https://oramavr.com/), and I keep
[publishing](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JfdNPDkAAAAJ&hl=en)
when the work is worth sharing.

I care about AI that earns people's trust, especially in health, and about
small teams that ship.
