### Results I am proud of

<ul class="results">
  <li><strong>Self-supervised learning in a recommender serving billions of requests</strong>
    At Taboola I proposed the R&amp;D initiative, led a new team of five from prototype to production, and landed +0.8% CTR and +0.5% RPM under strict latency budgets.</li>
  <li><strong>A transformer tokenizer for 3D meshes with 75% sequence compression</strong>
    At ORamaVR I architected the company's core ML stack, led instruction-based 3D editing with Geometric Algebra and LLMs, and shipped RAG assistants that guide surgical procedures in VR.</li>
  <li><strong>Federated learning across hospitals, in production</strong>
    At CHUV I extended a length-of-stay prediction system with privacy-preserving training across institutions and made the whole pipeline reproducible.</li>
  <li><strong>A multivariate unimodality test, published at UAI 2025</strong>
    With Aristidis Likas, a test that scales Hartigan's dip statistic to high dimensions and doubles as a cluster-count estimator. Open source as mudpod.</li>
</ul>
